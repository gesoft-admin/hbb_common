# Licensing note

This repository carries no `LICENSE` file, no `license` key in `Cargo.toml`,
and no copyright headers. That is how it arrives from upstream
(`rustdesk/hbb_common`), not something this fork removed.

`hbb_common` is not a standalone crate. It is a submodule and workspace member
of the RustDesk source tree, compiled into the binaries that tree produces and
distributed only as part of them. The licence that governs the work as a whole
is stated there:

* Upstream: <https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk> — AGPL-3.0, in `LICENCE`.
* Our fork: <https://github.com/gesoft-admin/rustdesk> — same, plus
  `LICENCE-COMPLIANCE.md`, which explains how we treat this repository's
  silence and what we do and do not claim about it.

This note points at that discussion. It does not itself declare a licence for
this repository, and nothing here should be read as a statement about the
licence status of historical `hbb_common` code.
